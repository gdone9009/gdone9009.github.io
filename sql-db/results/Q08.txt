설명: 모든 카테고리와 해당하는 도서 목록 조회 (LEFT JOIN)
SQL:
SELECT c.category_name, b.title AS book_title FROM categories c LEFT JOIN books b ON c.category_id = b.category_id ORDER BY c.category_id;
